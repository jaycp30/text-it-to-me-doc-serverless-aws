2. What the service does

You upload one or more photographs of a prescription. An AI model reads them and extracts the medications, doses and timing. The service then builds a dose schedule and sends reminders by email or SMS at the times it has worked out, in the timezone you select.

3. The AI can get it wrong

Prescriptions are frequently handwritten, abbreviated, or hard to photograph. The AI model that reads yours can and sometimes will misread it — a dose, a frequency, a drug name, or a date.

You are responsible for checking the schedule the app produces against your actual prescription and your pharmacist's instructions before relying on it. If the two disagree, your prescription is correct and the app is wrong.
