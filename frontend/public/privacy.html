Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Read this first: this app handles your prescription and medication information. That is health data, and it is treated as the most sensitive category of personal data. This page explains exactly what is collected, who processes it, and how long it is kept.

Text it To Me Doc does not provide medical advice. Always follow the instructions given by your doctor or pharmacist.

1. Who runs this service

Text it To Me Doc is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual developer based in the Philippines. It is not run by a company, and it is not a medical provider.

For anything in this policy, including requests about your data, contact: jay.clemen@outlook.com

2. There are no accounts

You never sign up, and there is no password. When you first use the app, your browser generates a random identifier and stores it locally on your device. That identifier is what links your prescription to your reminders.

This has three consequences worth understanding:

  • The same person using a phone and a laptop appears as two unrelated users, with two unrelated sets of data.
  • If you clear your browser storage, that identifier is gone permanently and you can no longer reach your own records from the app. If that happens, email the address above and your data can still be deleted on request.
  • The identifier is random and not linked to your name — but because it is stored alongside your email address or phone number, it is still treated as personal data under this policy.

3. What is collected, and why

DataWhy it is collected
Photographs of your prescription To read the medications, doses and instructions using an AI model
Medication details extracted from that photograph To build your dose schedule and the reminders sent to you
Your email address or phone number To send the reminders you asked for. Only the one you choose is collected
Your timezone To send each reminder at the correct local time
Messages you type into the in-app chat To answer questions about your own prescription
Your IP address and browser information Recorded in server logs, and used for bot protection on public endpoints

There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. No data is sold or shared for marketing.

4. Legal basis for using your data

Health data — your explicit consent
Your prescription photograph and the medication details taken from it are special category health data. These are processed only on the basis of the explicit consent you give before uploading, and you can withdraw that consent at any time (see section 8).
Contact details and timezone — performance of a contract
Your email address or phone number and your timezone are needed to deliver the reminder service you asked for. Without them the service cannot work.
Server logs and bot protection — legitimate interests
Limited technical data is processed to keep the service running and to stop automated abuse of the public endpoints.

5. Who else processes your data

Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Hosts the entire service — storage, database, email and SMS delivery, and server logs. All of it runs in the AWS Asia Pacific (Tokyo) region.
Amazon Bedrock (an AWS service)
Your prescription photograph and your chat messages are sent to an AI model (Claude) to extract medication details and answer your questions. This runs inside the same AWS account and region, using a Japan-resident inference profile, so the images are not sent to a separate company. AWS does not use this data to train models.
Cloudflare
Provides the "verify you are human" check on public endpoints. Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic browser signals when that check runs. It does not receive your prescription or contact details.
Your mobile network operator
If you choose SMS reminders, the message and your phone number pass through the mobile networks that deliver it, as with any text message.

6. Where your data is processed

Everything is processed in Japan, in the AWS Asia Pacific (Tokyo) region. The European Commission has recognised Japan as providing an adequate level of data protection, so no additional safeguard is required for data moving from the EEA or the UK to Japan.

The Cloudflare bot check is the one exception: it is operated globally, and your IP address may be processed outside Japan.

7. How long your data is kept

DataKept for
Prescription photographs 75 days, then automatically deleted
Extracted medication details and your schedule 1 year, then automatically deleted
Your email address or phone number 1 year, stored with the schedule it belongs to
Pending reminders Deleted as soon as they are sent, or when you stop reminders
Server logs 30 days

These limits run automatically. You do not have to wait for them: "Delete my data" removes everything immediately, and is described in section 8 below.

8. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • Get a copy of the data held about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Have your data deleted
  • Withdraw your consent to health data processing, which stops the service
  • Object to, or ask to restrict, how your data is used
  • Receive your data in a portable format

Stopping reminders and deleting your data are two different things. The unsubscribe link in any reminder email, and the "Cancel reminders" control in the app, stop notifications being sent but keep your schedule so you can restart it. Neither deletes anything.

To actually delete your data, use "Delete my data" — in the Reminders tab of the app, or from the link in the footer of any reminder email. It asks you to confirm, then permanently removes your prescription images, the medications read from them, your email address or phone number, and every upcoming reminder. It cannot be undone and we cannot recover the data afterwards.

You are identified by an ID stored in your browser, so if you clear your site data or switch device you may no longer be able to reach your own records. In that case, email jay.clemen@outlook.com and your data will still be deleted on request. Any request sent by email is answered within one month.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also complain to your national data protection authority. In the Philippines, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission.

9. How your data is protected

  • All traffic between your device and the service uses HTTPS
  • Prescription images are encrypted at rest, in storage with no public access
  • Reminder and schedule data is held in access-controlled AWS databases
  • Server logs do not record your contact details or medication names

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach affects your data and puts you at risk, you will be told without undue delay.

10. Children

This service is not intended for use by children under 18. If a parent or guardian is managing medication on a child's behalf, the adult is responsible for the information they upload.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes to how health data is handled will be notified before they take effect, where there is a way to reach you.

12. Contact
